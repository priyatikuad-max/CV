Introduction

About

I am an architect who designs across scales, from a single detail to the city, creating spaces that serve people and belong to their place.

I trained at Khulna University in Bangladesh and spent more than three years in practice in Dhaka and Chattogram, taking projects from first concept through working drawings to the building site, from a community library in Rajshahi to a fourteen-storey residential building.

In 2022 I moved to Germany for a Master’s degree at Anhalt University of Applied Sciences. My studies broadened how I see architecture: from giving war-damaged buildings in Berlin a new life, to understanding how traditional timber-frame buildings are made, adding careful analysis to my design process.

Whether working on housing, public, religious or cultural buildings, I think about context, light, material and the way people use space. The projects in this portfolio, from urban strategies to single buildings, show how I think, draw and build.
